Why we are adding pages

Right now the site comes up on Google when somebody already knows the shop by name, and you rank near the top for that. Where it does not come up is when a stranger searches something like "tattoo shop Wilmington." Google can only match you to searches you have actually written about, and at the moment the site has about a page of writing on it in total.

These four pages fix that. Each one covers something real that people search for.

Custom Tattoos →

Fine line, color, black and grey, illustrative.

Piercings →

Ears, curated lobes, nose, navel and more.

Scarification →

Barely anyone near Wilmington offers this.

Vegan Tattoos →

Vegan inks as standard, never an upsell.

About the yellow boxes

You will see yellow boxes scattered through the pages. Those are the spots where we need your answer, and they are the only thing standing between these and going live.

They cover things only you can tell us: your prices, your deposit, your age and ID rules, your aftercare, healing times, and what jewelry you stock. We deliberately did not guess at any of it. A made up price or a wrong age rule sitting on your website could cause you real headaches, and we would rather leave a gap than put words in your mouth.

Everything else is already written. Answer the yellow boxes and we drop your words straight in.

Easiest way to answer

Do not type it all out. Open each page, read the yellow boxes, and send back a voice memo talking through them. Honestly the way you describe your own work is usually better than anything we would write, so the more you just talk, the better these end up reading.

Nothing here is live yet

This is on our site, not yours. Your actual website is untouched and running exactly as it was. Nothing changes until you have seen it and said go.
