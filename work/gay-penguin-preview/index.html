Why I am adding pages

Right now your site comes up on Google when somebody already knows the shop by name, and you rank near the top for that. Where it does not come up is when a stranger searches something like "tattoo shop Wilmington." Google can only match you to searches you have actually written about, and at the moment the site has about a page of writing on it in total.

These four pages fix that. Each one covers something real that people search for.

A few things I still need from you

These pages are written and ready. There are a handful of things I left off on purpose, because they are yours to state and I did not want to guess and put something wrong on your site. Send these over and I will add them in and get everything live.

What to send

Money. Your shop minimum, rough ranges you are happy to publish, piercing prices, and whether the jewelry is included. Deposit amount, and does it come off the total.

Rules. Minimum age for tattoos and for piercings, what ID you need, and anything different with a parent there. Walk ins or appointments, and which days are realistically walk in friendly.

The work. Your aftercare sheet exactly as you give it to clients, the healing times you quote per spot, what jewelry you stock and in what metal, and whether you do cover ups.

The crew. One line each on Brian, Maya, Sabrina and Rose. Who is known for what.

Easiest way to send it

Do not type all that out. Just send a voice memo talking through it. Honestly the way you describe your own work is usually better than anything I would write, so the more you just talk, the better these end up reading.

Nothing here is live yet

This is on my site, not yours. Your actual website is untouched and running exactly as it was. Nothing changes until you have seen it and told me to go ahead.
