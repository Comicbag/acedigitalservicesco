Who does the piercing

Rose is the shop's piercer, and also tattoos. That matters more than it sounds, because a piercer who works in the same room every day builds a feel for what actually heals well on different bodies, rather than just placing a needle where you point.

If you are not sure what you want yet, that is a completely normal way to walk in. Bring a photo, or just point at your ear and say you want something there. Working out placement together is part of the job.

What "curated lobes" actually means

You will see the phrase everywhere on Instagram and almost nowhere explained, so here it is plainly. A curated ear is when the piercings and the jewelry are planned as one arrangement rather than added one at a time over the years. Instead of three studs that happen to be near each other, you get a layout designed around the shape of your ear, with the metals, stone sizes and spacing chosen to work together.

It usually happens over more than one visit, because piercings need to heal before the next one goes in nearby. The planning is the part people underestimate and the part that makes the finished ear look intentional.

Jewelry

The jewelry matters as much as the piercing. The wrong metal in a fresh hole is one of the most common reasons a piercing gets angry and stays angry, and cheap jewelry is the usual culprit.

Needs Brian

What is stocked, and in what materials? Implant grade titanium, gold, anything else? Is the jewelry price included in the piercing price or separate? Can people bring their own? This is a real decision point for anyone with sensitive skin, and "quality jewelry" on its own does not answer it.
