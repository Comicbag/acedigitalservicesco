Our Story

What is in a name? Why The Gay Penguin

We took over a Wilmington shop that had been here for 25 years, and then we gave it a name that makes it obvious who is welcome.

🏳️‍🌈 Since we took over📍 Market St🐧 Yes, really

The shop that was here before us

Before it was The Gay Penguin, this shop was Cadillac Custom Tattoo and Piercing, and it had been part of Wilmington for more than 25 years. It passed through a few different sets of hands over that time before it came to us. That history is not something we wanted to paper over. A shop that has been tattooing the same city for a quarter of a century has put ink on an enormous number of people around here, and plenty of our regulars first walked through this door under the old sign.

If you have followed us on Facebook you may have noticed the page address still carries the old name. That is not a mistake and it is not worth undoing. It is simply where the shop's history lives.

Why we changed the name

The old name was catchy, but it did not describe us. We know a great deal more about nipple piercings and fine line tattoos than we will ever know about radiator hoses. Keeping it would have meant spending the next decade explaining that we are not, in fact, a garage.

So we started making lists. We tried out a few of the obvious ideas, the safe ones that every shop in every beach town could have used, and none of them fit. What we kept coming back to was that the name needed to do two jobs at once. It had to sound like the actual vibe inside the shop, and it had to make it obvious, before anyone walked in, that everybody is welcome here.

Where the penguin came from

Wilmington is two towns layered on top of each other. It is a beach town full of visitors who are here for a good week, and it is a college town with UNCW right down the road. We wanted something that suited both, something with a bit of warmth and a bit of humour to it.

The Gay Penguin turned up on a list next to a lot of safer, duller options. Choosing it felt like a risk at the time. We decided the risk was the point, because a name that takes a clear stand for our LGBTQ+ family is a name that tells you what kind of room you are walking into before you get there.

It turned out to be the best filter we could have asked for

The name has quietly done more work than any advertising we could have paid for. It pre screens. People who would not be kind in this space tend to read the sign and keep driving, and everyone else walks in already knowing they are welcome.

The result is that every single person who comes through the door feels like family, whether they are here for a nose piercing, a butterfly on the ankle, a full back piece, or genuinely just to look around because they liked the name. We could not be prouder of that, and we would make the same call again.

What we do with it now

We are a small studio on Market St with four artists. Brian owns the shop and tattoos. Maya and Sabrina tattoo. Rose pierces and tattoos. Everything we use is vegan, and we try to be a real part of the city rather than a business that happens to sit in it, which means sponsoring local events and fundraisers where we can, and offering a discount to students, teachers, and first responders.
