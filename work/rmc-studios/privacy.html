Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, and who can see it.

A note while this site is in preview: this is a rebuilt version of rmcstudios.com, prepared by Ace Digital and not yet published at that address. RMC Studios should read it and tell us anything that is wrong before it goes live. Last updated: 21 September 2026.

What we collect, and only when you give it to us

There are three forms on this site. The class information form collects the student's name and age, the class type, the days and times that suit you, the parent's name, email and phone number, the student's experience, a location and anything you choose to tell us. The subscribe form collects an email address. The Club House form collects what you type into it. Each also records the page you sent it from and your browser type.

Visit counts

The site counts page views so we can see which pages people use. It records the page, the site that sent you here if there was one, and a random number stored in your browser so repeat visits are not counted as new people. The visit counter sets no cookies and records no IP address. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. Videos play from YouTube only after you press play, and YouTube then receives your IP address.

Where it goes, and who reads it

Everything you send is kept in a database that Ace Digital runs for RMC Studios. It is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers. Only the RMC Studios team and Ace Digital can read it, and RMC Studios can have a full export at any time.

Leaving the mailing list, or removing your details

Call (908) 280-0398 or email scheduling@rmcstudios.com and we will remove you.
