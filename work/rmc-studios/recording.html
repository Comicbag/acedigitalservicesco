RMC & Inspiration Audio

Recording • Mixing • Mastering • Filming

RMC Studios now partners with Inspiration Audio, a nonprofit recording studio that offers premier recording, mixing, mastering, production, and multimedia services to students and local musicians.

For more information about booking a recording session, music production classes, livestreaming, or filming services, please visit their website:

Inspiration Audio

Garwood Live Room

The Garwood Location currently hosts Inspiration Audio's recording and filming room, equipped for professional video and audio recording for solo artists all the way up to full bands. Current RMC students also have full access to the recording studio to record original songs, content for their social media, or audition materials.

Students can also register through Inspiration Audio for private mentorship sessions where you will be assisted, weekly, in the composition and arranging original music.

Session, podcast, or audition performances can also be professionally filmed, edited, or livestreamed at the Garwood location.

Rahway Studio

The Rahway location offers a more intimate setting for collaborating with the engineer and recording solo tracks.

Local artists will find a comfortable, professional environment to record and collaborate on their current projects.

Inspiration Audio's goal is to make you every artist feel completely in control and excited about their work.

Each engineer is an active musician that wants nothing more than to help the true voice of the artist and their music be heard.
