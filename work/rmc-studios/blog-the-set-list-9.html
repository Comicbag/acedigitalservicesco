WT Music & Son’s Summer and Fall 2K19

“Beat My Rental” Program

Tis the season for graduation parties, barbecues, summer vacations and outdoor sports. However, for some parents, another adventure has begun and that’s the voyage into your child’s band and/or orchestra music education. Teachers will be handing out some information about renting instruments sometime between June and October. This can represent both an exciting AND confusing time for students and parents alike.

However, if you are an RMC student, you have a leg up and a friendly voice of reason on your side through this process. WT Music & Son is offering their knowledge and expertise of the school band and orchestra rental industry to parents as well as a unique rental program that offers parents a choice rather than just a singular option and application to fill out.

Schedule some time to discuss your school’s music program with president and owner of WT Music & Son, Chris Tarnowski. Chris is the third generation of his family’s 60 year old musical instrument rental, repair and sales business. His family has seen everything in the world of brass, woodwind and orchestral music.

You can show the rental application distributed by your music teacher to Chris and he can help you with the following:

1.) Musical instruments in general. Chris’ family has watched thousands of students get their start in music over the years and knows everything there is to know about getting started on each instrument. Unfortunately, there is a flood of instruments in the market right now that you DON’T want your child to be playing on. Chris can help you avoid common pitfalls and mistakes parents make.

2.) Rental programs and why you rent versus buy an instrument. There is a lot of fine print and language in rental applications and the world of music can be somewhat overwhelming and challenging, even to those parents that consider themselves “music minded”. Chris Tarnowski can help you see the forest from the trees on everything.

3.) Chris can show you how WT Music & Son can essentially “beat your rental” by explaining how his rental program beats the rental application you have been given in terms of price, quality and service.

All consultations about musical instruments and rentals are free of charge and there’s no obligation to rent with WT Music & Son after your consultation with Chris.

The family at WT Music & Son knows you deserve an enriching and supportive environment for your family’s music education. Don’t delay. Call WT Music & Son today!
