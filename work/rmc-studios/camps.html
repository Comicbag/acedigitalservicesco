Summer Camps at RMC Studios

Ready to take your music to the next level?

RMC Studios’ Summer Camps aren’t just about playing music, they’re about living it. Whether you're a beginner looking to explore your passion or a seasoned player eager for the next challenge, our camps give you real-world music experiences that go beyond the classroom.

At RMC Studios, we don’t do “cookie-cutter” camps. Every session is designed to push your creativity, sharpen your skills, and fuel your passion for music. You’ll work one-on-one with expert musicians, jam with fellow students, and walk away with real-world tools and industry secrets you can use for life.

All camps are taught in-person at our Garwood, NJ location.

Songwriting & Recording Camp

Ages 8-16

Got a song idea? Let’s turn it into a professionally recorded masterpiece! Whether you’re an aspiring songwriter or a seasoned musician, our Songwriting & Recording Camp gives you the tools, mentorship, and studio time to bring your music to life.

  • Come in with an idea, leave with a fully produced, ready-to-release song.
  • Experience hands-on studio recording and learn from expert recording artists.
  • Master the art of songwriting, from lyrics to melody to arrangement.
  • (Advanced Songwriters Session, extra training & an extra day to create.

No matter your instrument or experience level, this camp is for you. Let’s make music that’s ready for the world!

Rockshop Camp

Ages 8-15

Dreaming of being in a rock band? This is your chance! Whether you’re picking up an instrument for the first time or already jamming at home, Rockshop is where you’ll learn, play, and perform like a pro. Get hands-on experience with real band dynamics, new instruments, and live performance skills, all while having a blast with fellow musicians!

  • Jam out on your favorite songs with your bandmates.
  • Learn teamwork & camaraderie, because great bands play together!
  • Design & make your own band T-shirt to rock onstage.
  • Level up your stage presence with fun performance exercises.
  • Explore different instruments and find your perfect sound.
  • End the week with a live concert for family & friends!

No experience? No problem! All skill levels are welcome. Just bring your passion for music, and we’ll take care of the rest.

Superstars Stage Camp

Ages 8-15

Calling all future Broadway stars and concert headliners! Whether you're prepping for an audition or just love the stage, this performance-focused camp will elevate your skills in singing, acting, and stage presence. With expert coaching and hands-on training, you'll build confidence, refine your craft, and shine like never before!

  • Get one-on-one vocal training and pick the perfect song for auditions.

K-Pop Demon Hunters Sing-A-Long Camp

Ages 7-11

Whether you’re already obsessed with K-Pop Demon Hunters or just love to sing along to your favorite songs, this camp is your chance to step into the spotlight and bring the music to life.

Spend three high-energy days singing the hit songs inspired by the show, learning key lyrics in Korean, and building the confidence to perform with flair and attitude.

  • Sing your heart out to the songs you can’t stop playing.
  • Learn beginner-friendly vocal techniques to strengthen your voice and expand your range.
  • Learn to speak & sing in Korean
  • Make new friends who love K-pop just as much as you do.
  • End the week with a performance for family and friends.

This camp is perfect for beginners and experienced singers alike. No prior training needed! Just bring your love of music, your energy, and your inner superstar.

Spots are limited, so grab the mic and join us for a week of music, friendship, and K-pop fun.
