Our Story

Educating. Guiding. Inspiring.

Celebrating 20 Years of Music Education

Since 2005, RMC Studios has been a place where students of all ages and skill levels discover their passion for music. Our journey began in the basement of our founder EJ Gaub's home in Rahway, NJ. As neighbors and friends joined, what started as a small effort quickly grew into something extraordinary. Today, RMC Studios calls Garwood, NJ, home and is announcing their return to Rahway, NJ where they will be opening their second location.

At RMC, we know the key to long-lasting music education is keeping lessons engaging and tailored to each student's interests. Whether you dream of pursuing a career in music or simply want to play your favorite songs, we help make music a lifelong joy.

We believe performing is a cornerstone of music education. From solo recitals to full-band concerts, we offer students real-world performance experiences to build confidence and make learning fun. Our flagship Rockshop program puts students into bands where they play songs they love, learn teamwork, and take the stage at venues like the Stone Pony and House of Independents.

Music is more than lessons, it's community, creativity, and a lifelong connection to something greater.

Let us help you or your child start your music journey today.
