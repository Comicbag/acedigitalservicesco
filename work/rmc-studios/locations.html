RMC Garwood

flagship location

RMC Studios' second home is Garwood, NJ where we have lived at 207 Center Street.

This facility houses 10 lesson rooms and is located in the heart of downtown Garwood.

With close access to Westfield, Cranford, Clark, Scotch-Plains, and Garwood, NJ, this location is ideal for in-studio and in-home lessons in the area.

Directions

Located across the street from the Garwood Post Office just 2 blocks south of South Ave on Center Street. Enter using the front or back doors. There is a small lobby where parents can wait during lessons.

There is a small municipal parking lot right next door to our building. Parking is free after 6pm. There is also ample free street parking available.
