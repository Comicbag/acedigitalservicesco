RMC Drumline

Tuesdays 4:30-5:30 PM

The RMC Drumline is the place to be for drummers to expand on their percussive knowledge, learn basic Drumline drills & commands, improve their stick technique, and learn percussion arrangements to popular tunes.

There is always a drum part for all skill levels, so inquire today to come and try it out.
