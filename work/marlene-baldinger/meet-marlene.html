Meet Marlene.

Marlene Baldinger has lived in Lebanon Borough for 36 years. She is a registered nurse, a mother of two, and the current Council President. Before she ever ran for office, neighbors knew her as the woman who ran through town and stopped to talk. That is where her theme comes from: running for what’s important to you.

When she first ran nine years ago, council seats here went uncontested. She believed residents deserved a choice, so she gave them one, and she has knocked on every door in the borough each campaign since.

And yes, the last line of the list is real. Every interviewer asks about it.

Personal running stats

  • Lebanon Council Committee Memberships: Public Safety, Office of Emergency Management, Communication, Liaison to the School Board, Board of Health, Historical Committee
