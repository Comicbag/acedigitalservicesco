Anyone can print a promise. Here is what happened to the ones Marlene printed.

Delivered · Aug 19, 2026

Goal two on her 2023 card: livestream council meetings and put them on the borough website.

She was the only member of council advocating for it, and it took nine years. On August 19, 2026, Lebanon Borough held its first livestreamed council meeting. Meetings can now be watched live or afterward, and the land use board is being added as well.
