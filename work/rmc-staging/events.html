Garwood Rocks

Garwood's annual street festival closes Center Street for a car show, vendors, a kids' zone and an all day live stage. The 2025 edition was its eleventh year and expected close to 10,000 people. RMC Staging's stage and truss were on Center Street for Garwood Rocks 2024, and RMC Studios' student bands have played the festival for years.

Attendance and history from TAPinto's 2025 festival coverage. The 2024 photos are RMC Staging's own.

RMC Fest

RMC Studios' own festival at Unami Park in Cranford, where its students play their first shows, ran on the same stage and truss on the grass in 2024. Same roof, same banners, a very different crowd.
