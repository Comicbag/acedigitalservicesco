Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, and who can see it. Written in plain language, because a policy nobody reads protects nobody.

A note while this site is in preview: this is a rebuilt version of rmcstaging.com, prepared by Ace Digital and not yet published at that address. It describes how the new site works. RMC Staging should read it and tell us anything that is wrong before it goes live. Last updated: 20 September 2026.

Who we are

RMC Staging is an event rental business at 207 Center Street, Garwood, New Jersey. You can reach us at (908) 800-0466. If you want anything on this page explained, or you want your information removed, that is the number to call. Confirm with EJ: an email address for privacy requests.

What we collect, and only when you give it to us

There is one form on this site, the quote request. It collects exactly what it shows you: your name, email address, phone number, the date and place of your event, what you need, and whatever you write in the message box. Only the email address is required.

Where it goes, and who reads it

Everything you send goes to a database that RMC Staging owns, running on a server that Ace Digital operates and maintains for them. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you. There are no advertising networks on this site and no accounts to create.

The people who can read it are the RMC Staging team, and Ace Digital as the company that builds and maintains the site. Nobody else.

Other companies whose things appear on this site

Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, which means your browser requests two font files from Google. That is the only third party request the site makes. The setup video is hosted on this site, not on Instagram.

How long we keep things

Quote requests are kept so RMC Staging can follow up and, if you book, run the event. Confirm with EJ: how long to keep past requests, for example two years. Ask and we will delete yours sooner.
