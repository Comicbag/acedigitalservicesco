Modular stage

An All-Terrain indoor and outdoor stage system, built from 4 by 4 panels to whatever footprint your event needs.

Every rental includes the stage skirt, stairs and railings. Heights available: 24, 32, 40 and 48 inches. Each leg adjusts on its own, so the deck sits level on uneven ground.

Any surface, any size

The 4 by 4 panels and adjustable leg heights mean we can make almost every configuration on any surface. Grass, gravel, a parking lot, a gym floor: the stage goes where the event is, and we build it to the size the event needs, up to 16 by 20 feet.

The deck has a water resistant industrial finish.

The load rating and per leg adjustment are from the manufacturer, All-Terrain Staging by IntelliStage. Everything else on this page is from RMC Staging's own listing.
