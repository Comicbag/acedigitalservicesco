Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone who lands on it.

What we have done

This site is built to meet WCAG 2.1 AA where we can. In practice that means a skip link to the main content, a single clear heading order on every page, visible focus outlines on everything you can tab to, text that meets AA contrast against its background (including the black on orange buttons, which is why they are not white on orange), alt text on images that carry meaning, form labels that are real labels rather than placeholder text, and full keyboard operation including the menu.

Animation on this site is limited to gentle fade ins and a short muted video. If your device is set to reduce motion, the fade ins do not run.
