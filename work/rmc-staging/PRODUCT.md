# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People putting on an event in and around Union County, New Jersey, who need a stage, a truss and a PA delivered and set up: a borough recreation committee running a summer concert series (Garwood Rocks is the documented example), a school or PTA running a graduation or field day, a wedding or a company picnic, a festival or a block party. They are usually not production people. They have a date, a lawn or a parking lot, and a rough headcount, and they want to know three things fast: what the stage is, whether it fits, and how to get a quote. Most arrive on a phone from a search, a referral, or the RMC Studios connection.

## Product Purpose

The website is the quote funnel for RMC Staging, EJ Gaub's event rental arm run out of the RMC Studios building at 207 Center Street, Garwood. It exists to turn "we need a stage for the 14th" into a completed quote request that lands in EJ and Liz's dashboard, and to answer the sizing and power questions that otherwise become a phone call. Success is a quote request with the event date, location, headcount and what they need already filled in, submitted from a phone, with no back and forth about whether the stage fits.

Everything on it is sourced from their own published material (specs, gear list, photos, partner brands, address, response promise) or clearly marked as a fact to confirm with EJ. No invented pricing, no invented reviews.

## Brand Personality

Sturdy, hands on, road crew. The voice is someone who has hauled the stage into a parking lot at 6am and had the band on it by noon. Plain sentences, real dimensions, no event-industry gloss. Confidence comes from the specs and the photos of the actual stage in use, not from adjectives. Three words: sturdy, direct, local.

## Anti-references

- The current rmcstaging.com: a Wix template with white text on a near-white hero, a blank image slot, a clipped button, a unit error in the generator specs and a 2024 copyright. The rebuild must never look templated or half finished.
- Generic "event production" agency sites: purple gradients, drone footage of festivals they did not do, "elevate your event" copy, stock crowds. Their own site already leans on "elevate"; the rebuild drops it.
- The sister site treatment. Inspiration Audio is dark, red, Archivo. RMC Staging shares an owner, not a look. Same build kit underneath, different skin: it should read as a rental yard, not a recording studio.
- AI tells: em dashes, eyebrow labels over every section, three identical icon cards, numbered section markers, mono type as a "technical" costume, hand drawn SVG illustrations.

## Design Principles

- Specs are the content. The 4x4 panels, the four leg heights, the 16 by 20 maximum, the 7000 watt generator: these are the reasons to book. Show them large and legible, in their own units, never buried in prose or a hairline table.
- Show the real stage. Every photo is their stage, their truss, their gear, at their events. Manufacturer press shots may fill the gear list where they have no photo, labelled as such. Nothing generated, nothing stock.
- One job per page, one ask per page. The quote request is the only call to action, worded the same way everywhere.
- Built for a phone in a parking lot. Big touch targets, specs readable at arm's length, the quote form usable one handed.
- Honest gaps. Anything they have not published (prices, delivery radius, lead time, insurance) is a marked placeholder for EJ, never a guess.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the floor, same as the Inspiration Audio build: skip link, one h1 per page, visible focus on everything, AA contrast on all text including on the orange, real labels on every form control, keyboard operable menu, reduced motion respected. Orange on white must be checked for contrast at every size it is used; white on orange is only for large bold text.
