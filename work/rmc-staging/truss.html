Truss system

Aluminum box truss for larger shows and productions. Roof, backdrop and banner wings included in every rental.

Global Truss SQ-4112

Square aluminum box truss with diagonal bracing, built for structural support at music events. It goes up over the stage at full height and width, and it carries whatever the show needs above the band: the roof, the backdrop, the banner, and the speakers if you want them flown.

The black mesh roof and backdrop come with every truss rental. Wings for hanging an event banner are built in. Speaker mounts are available on request.

Tube and section dimensions are from Global Truss. The 18 by 21 foot maximum and what is included are RMC Staging's own listing.
