About RMC Staging

An intimate, New Jersey based team with over 10 years of experience, dedicated to making sure your event runs smoothly, whatever its size or type.

Who we are

We're RMC Staging, an event rental service here to help make your next event stand out and also not break the budget. We know how stressful planning and executing an event can be, and that's why we're here: to help your event run as smoothly as possible and look great.

Our mission is to enhance the event atmosphere and empower organizers with reliable and professional event elements. We pride ourselves on making your event stand out through timely execution and exceptional customer service.

The paragraphs above are RMC Staging's own words, from rmcstaging.com and its Instagram.

The RMC family

RMC Staging runs out of the RMC Studios building at 207 Center Street in Garwood. RMC Studios is the music school EJ Gaub founded in 2005, with over 500 students today. RMC Staging's stage and truss were on the grass for RMC Fest 2024, the school's own festival.

Rental enquiries are handled by Liz Robbins, who also runs rockshops, events and rentals for RMC Studios.

Confirm with EJ: the year RMC Staging started, how it began, and the crew size on an average build.

How a rental goes

  1. You ask

    Send the date, the place and a rough headcount through the quote form. We reply within 24 hours.

  2. We size it

    Stage footprint and height, whether you need the truss roof, how much PA, whether there is power on site.

  3. We build it

    The crew arrives, sets up and sound checks before your first guest. Confirm with EJ: typical setup lead time before doors.

  4. We take it down

    When the event is over we strike everything and leave the site the way we found it. Confirm with EJ: same night or next morning?

Partners we build with

Global Truss, All-Terrain Staging, Honda Generators, Electro-Voice and Behringer. The gear on the list is the gear in the trailer.
