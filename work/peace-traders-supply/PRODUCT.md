# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
New Jersey firearm buyers within driving distance of Lebanon Plaza on Route 22 East: people who
research a specific gun online, then buy in person because NJ law routes every sale through a
licensed dealer. Many already follow the shop on Instagram or Facebook. They arrive on a phone,
often from a social post, and want three things fast: what is in the case right now, whether the
shop can do what they need (AOWs and Others, transfers, special orders), and how to get there.

## Product Purpose
Peace Traders Supply is a licensed FFL and SOT gun store that opened in summer 2026 in Lebanon
Plaza, Lebanon NJ. Its web presence today is a "Coming Soon" domain and two social accounts. This
site is the store's front door: a catalog of what they post, category pages for browsing by type,
the story of the shop, a plain New Jersey buying guide, and the way in. Success = a buyer calls or
walks in already knowing what they want, and the shop stops answering the same questions on
Instagram DMs.

## Brand Personality
Precise, quiet, well made. Three words: engraved, unhurried, exact. The shop's own identity is a
classic serif monogram (PTS with a cartridge inside the P) on a white sign, and an interior with
white slatwall, glass cases and a warm acid stained floor. The site should feel like that counter:
dark, calm, the product lit, nothing shouting. Emotion: confidence that this is the serious shop.

## Anti-references
- Tacky generated "atmosphere" imagery, stock showrooms, fake textures. Corey killed these on sight.
- Template one pagers: uniform card grids, eyebrows over every section, three feature tiles.
- Cropped product photography, or thumbnails standing in for photos. Every gun shows whole.
- Tacticool cliches: camo, distressed textures, aggressive red, military stencil type.
- Big box retailer energy (price tags, badges, sale banners). This is a counter, not a warehouse.

## Design Principles
- The product is the design: their photos, uncropped, are the loudest element on every page.
- Their words, their facts: captions verbatim, hours from Google, rules from the NJSP FAQ. Unknown
  facts get a visible "[Confirm with Kyle]" instead of a guess.
- One accent, used like a stamp: hunter orange on the ampersand, the numbers and the primary CTA.
- Motion is a press and a reveal, never a show: fast ease out, nothing loops, reduced motion honored.
- A phone at the counter is the primary screen: the number, the hours and the case are one thumb away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast on the dark theme (bone on charcoal), keyboard operable lightbox and menu, visible
focus rings, real alt text on every photo, reduced motion alternative for every animation, the Ace
Digital assist menu (a11y.js) on every page.
