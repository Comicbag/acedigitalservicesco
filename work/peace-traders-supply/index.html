AOWs & Others.

Much of what Peace Traders posts is built as an AOW or a Non NFA Other, the two categories behind the short configurations in the case.

AOW
Any other weapon, a category under the National Firearms Act. The ATF transfer tax on an AOW is $5.
Non NFA Other
A firearm configured to stay outside the National Firearms Act, so no tax stamp is involved.
SOT
Peace Traders Supply is a licensed SOT, so AOWs can be sold and transferred in store.

Buying a firearm in New Jersey.

The short version of the New Jersey State Police rules. The shop confirms current requirements when you visit.

  1. Get your Firearms Purchaser ID Card.

    New Jersey requires a Firearms Purchaser Identification Card before you can obtain a rifle or shotgun. Apply online through FARS with your local police department.

  2. Handguns need a permit for each one.

    Each handgun needs its own electronic Permit to Purchase a Handgun, also issued through FARS.

  3. The dealer runs your NICS check.

    Firearm transactions in New Jersey require a NICS background check completed by a licensed dealer at the time of transfer.
