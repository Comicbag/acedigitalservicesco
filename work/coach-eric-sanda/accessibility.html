Our commitment

Accessibility

Coach Eric SanDa wants everyone to be able to use this website, including visitors with disabilities.

This site is built and looked after to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, known as WCAG, version 2.1 at Level AA. That is the standard widely used for websites that work well for people of all abilities.

What has been done

  • Clear page structure and headings so the site is easy to follow.
  • Meaningful text descriptions for images.
  • Colour and contrast chosen to stay readable.
  • Full keyboard navigation for people who do not use a mouse.
  • Motion reduced automatically for anyone whose device asks for it.
  • An accessibility menu on every page, with text sizing, contrast and colour options, a dyslexia friendly font, reading aids, a larger cursor, and the option to pause motion or hide images.

Keeping it up

Accessibility is not a one time job. The site gets checked and improved as it changes.

If something is hard to use

If any part of this site is difficult for you, or you need information in another format, please get in touch and we will help you and put it right.
