Eric coaches around 80 private clients at UFC FIT in Wayne, where he is Assistant MMA Director, and puts in 30+ hours a week on the pads. Every slot is taken at the moment. When one opens it goes to the waitlist first, in order.

What a session is like

One to one or small group, depending on what you are after. Sessions are built around what you actually need rather than a fixed class plan, which is why the room has complete beginners and people who have been at it for years in it at the same time.

Expect to spend your first few sessions on stance and movement. It is not the exciting part and it is the part that makes everything after it work.

Eric competed at national level in SanDa, but he is not running fight camps here. Almost everyone on the floor is a regular person with a job. If competing is what you are after, ask him and he will tell you honestly whether it is a fit.
