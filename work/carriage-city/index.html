We are open. Find us at 1420 Irving Street in downtown Rahway, in the Rahway National building by the train station.

“We’re not trying to be a typical recording studio.”

Carriage City is being built as a content lab and creative hub, where artists can create, perform and connect, and where engineers can learn by actually doing. No gatekeeping. No pressure. Just real music and real collaboration.

It is a DIY studio powered by the New Jersey nonprofit Inspiration Audio, whose mission is to help any band with any budget. Every artist deserves to have their music cared for, with attention to detail and to the artist’s vision.
