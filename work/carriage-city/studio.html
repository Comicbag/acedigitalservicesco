The studio

We are Carriage City Studios. Located in Rahway, NJ, we are a studio dedicated to making epic records: a small but mighty team who want to help the bands of New Jersey showcase their music.

Our home: 1420 Irving Street

Carriage City’s home is in the Rahway National building, the blue building in the train station plaza. It was built in 1900 as the Rahway National Bank. In May 2024 the Rahway Planning Board approved the space for a recording and music lessons studio, and a performance space for music-related functions and rehearsals.

Rahway is known as the Carriage City of the World. The mural Glossblack painted on the building in 2025 honours that, and one of its walls is a horse and carriage for Carriage City.

The landlord’s fit-out of the space was under way as of March 2026. In the meantime we have tracked full bands in the unfinished space with packing blankets, duct tape and hope, and sessions continue at our current studio.

The fit-out is finished and the doors are open. Come by, or send a request to book a session.
