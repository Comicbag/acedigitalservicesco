Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, and who can see it.

A note while this site is in preview: this is a rebuilt version of carriagecitystudios.com, prepared by Ace Digital and not yet published at that address. Carriage City should read it and tell us anything that is wrong before it goes live. Last updated: 20 September 2026.

What we collect, and only when you give it to us

There are two forms on this site. The booking form collects your name, email address and message; only the email is required. The mailing list collects your name and email address. Each also records the page you sent it from and your browser type.

Visit counts

The site counts page views so we can see which pages people use. It records the page, the site that sent you here if there was one, and a random number stored in your browser so repeat visits are not counted as new people. The visit counter sets no cookies and records no IP address. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. Your browser also remembers whether it last saw the site as open or coming soon.

Where it goes, and who reads it

Everything you send is kept in a database that Ace Digital runs for Carriage City. It is not sold, rented or shared with advertisers. Only the Carriage City team and Ace Digital can read it, and Carriage City can have a full export at any time.

Leaving the mailing list, or removing your details

Email info@inspirationaudio.org or call (908) 900-0229 and we will remove you.
