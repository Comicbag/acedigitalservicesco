# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three kinds of people in and around Rahway, NJ, usually arriving on a phone from Instagram, a flyer, or the RMC Studios and Inspiration Audio sites:
- **Artists and bands** who want to record, rehearse or mix, and want to know what the room and the gear are before they book.
- **People planning an event** (a show, a party, a community night, a class) who want to know whether the space fits and how to ask.
- **Neighbours and fans** who want to hear what is coming: shows, open days, lessons, the opening itself.

## Product Purpose

The website is Carriage City Studios' front door: a recording studio, event space and community venue at 1420 Irving Street, Rahway, run by EJ Gaub and powered by RMC Studios. It exists to turn interest into a named, reachable request in EJ and Liz's dashboard: a session enquiry, an event enquiry, a lessons enquiry, a gear rental enquiry, or a mailing list signup. It replaces a Wix site that is mostly template: placeholder "Service 1/2/3" bookings at $19.99 that a stranger can confirm, a template About page with invented staff, and two different phone numbers.

Success: every enquiry arrives with enough detail to answer it in one reply, and nothing on the site is invented or out of date.

## Brand Personality

A Victorian carriage-house turned into a room for loud music: ornate gold crest, slate navy, "Est. MMXXIII", "Follow The Noise...". Warm, local, a little theatrical, never corporate. Three words: ornate, warm, loud.

## Anti-references

- The current Wix site: template pages, placeholder services, fake staff, a floating mailing list box.
- Generic "creative studio" sites: black backgrounds, neon, stock microphones, "unleash your sound".
- The sister sites' skins. Inspiration Audio is black and red; RMC Staging is orange and charcoal. Carriage City keeps its own navy and gold.
- AI tells: em dashes, eyebrow labels on every section, three identical icon cards, numbered markers, invented reviews or prices.

## Design Principles

- The crest is the brand. The gold on navy and the ornate frame come from their own logo; the site borrows its manners (rules, corners, small caps) sparingly and lets the photos and words carry the rest.
- Honest status. Whether the venue is open, what it costs and who runs sessions are stated only as far as they have published them; everything else is a visible "Confirm with EJ" placeholder.
- Every door leads to the dashboard. Each thing a visitor can ask for has one clear form, and every form posts to the RMC dashboard.
- One ask per page, worded the same everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: skip link, one h1 per page, visible focus, AA contrast (their gold #f2ac57 on their navy #2d4b62 is 4.6:1, so gold carries body-size text only at that pairing or darker), real labels, keyboard-operable menu, reduced motion respected.
