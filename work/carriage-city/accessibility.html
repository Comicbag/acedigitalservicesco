Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone who lands on it.

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 AA: a skip link to the main content, one clear heading order on every page, visible focus outlines, text designed to meet AA contrast (gold on navy is used only where it passes), alt text on images, real labels on every form field, and full keyboard operation including the menu. If your device asks for reduced motion, the fade ins do not run.

If any part of this site gets in your way, email info@inspirationaudio.org or call (908) 900-0229 and tell us what happened, and we will look at it.
