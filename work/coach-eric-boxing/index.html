What this actually is

SanDa, not another boxing course

There is no shortage of online striking courses. Almost all of them teach boxing or Muay Thai, filmed by someone who does not coach anybody in real life. This is a different art, taught by someone who is on the pads 30 hours a week.

01

Striking that includes the grab

SanDa is Chinese kickboxing, so it keeps the clinch, the throws and the takedowns alongside the hands and kicks. What happens if they grab you is part of the same system, not a different course.

02

Start at the actual beginning

Stance, guard, footwork, then the jab. Nothing assumes you have done this before, and nothing skips ahead because it looks better on camera.

03

Watch it as often as you need

The thing you cannot do in a live session is rewind it. Every breakdown stays in the library so you can go back to the same detail ten times.

Who trains here

Mostly people who have never thrown a punch

The room at UFC FIT Wayne is about half men and half women, and most of them walked in with no experience at all. Some wanted to get in shape and stayed for the technique. Some came specifically to learn how to protect themselves. A few had boxed years ago and wanted to fix habits they never got corrected.

The online program is built for the same mix. You do not need a gym, a partner, or anything beyond a bit of floor space to start.
