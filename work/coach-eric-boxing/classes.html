Defense

14 lessons

Self defense

12 lessons

Separate from the boxing, on purpose. This is about awareness, distance, and getting away, taught as decisions you make under stress rather than moves you memorise.

Questions

Before you sign up

I have never boxed. Is this going to be too advanced?

No. The Fundamentals track assumes you have never done any of this. The first lesson is how to stand.

Do I need equipment?

Not to start. Most of the Fundamentals and Self defense tracks need nothing but floor space. Gloves and a bag are useful later and there is a lesson on what to buy when you get there.

How much space do I need?

Enough to take two steps in any direction. A living room is fine.

Is this the same as training with you in person?

No, and it would be dishonest to say otherwise. In person you get corrected in real time, which is the part that matters most. The online program is the same material in the same order, and for most people it is the difference between learning properly and learning from whatever comes up on their feed.

Can I cancel?

Any time, and you keep access until the end of the month you have paid for.
