About

Eric Delpiano

Boxing and self defense coach in Wayne, New Jersey. Around 80 people train with him, roughly half of them women, and most of them had never thrown a punch before they walked in.

How I teach

Most people who come to me are not trying to fight anybody. They want to move better, feel stronger, and know what to do if something goes wrong. So that is what I teach. Boxing done properly, and self defense that actually works when you are frightened.

I do not rush people. Stance and footwork before punches, punches before combinations, combinations before anything that looks impressive. It is slower at the start and it is the reason people stay.

The goal is not to make you look like a boxer. It is to make you move like one without having to think about it.

Who I work with

Complete beginners. People coming back after years off. People who specifically want self defense and have no interest in the sport side of it at all. Teenagers and people well into their sixties. The room is mixed on purpose and nobody is the odd one out in it.

I am not a competition coach. I could take that work and I do not, because it is not what I enjoy and it is not what the people who train with me need. If you are preparing for a fight camp, I am probably not your guy, and I will tell you that honestly.

Why there is an online program

My schedule filled up. I kept getting asked and I kept having to say no, which I hated, and the people I had to turn away were exactly the people who needed the fundamentals most.

The online program is the same material, in the same order, taught the same way. It is not a substitute for having someone correct your elbow in real time. It is better than the alternative, which for most people is a YouTube video made by someone who has never taught a beginner in their life.
