Why this and not the other guy

There are a hundred online striking courses

Most of them teach you boxing, or Muay Thai, filmed by someone who does not coach anybody in real life. Three things here are actually different.

01

It is SanDa, not boxing

SanDa is Chinese kickboxing. Hands and kicks like anything else, but it also keeps the clinch, the throws and the takedowns. So when you ask what happens if they grab you, the answer is part of the same system instead of a different course.

02

Eric actually competed

Two US Open Advanced SanDa titles, two New England International golds, and a national silver taken with a fractured ankle. Everything taught here was tested against people who were trying to win.

03

He coaches for a living, right now

Around 80 private clients and 30+ hours a week on the pads at UFC FIT Wayne. This is not a training archive from someone who used to compete. It is what he is teaching this week.

How I teach

Most people who come to me are not trying to fight anybody. They want to move better, feel stronger, and know what to do if something goes wrong. So that is what I build for them.

I do not rush people. Stance and footwork before strikes, strikes before combinations, combinations before anything that looks impressive. It is slower at the start and it is the reason people stay.

I am not running fight camps. Almost everyone I work with is a regular person with a job, and the fact that I competed is there to make the coaching better, not to turn you into a competitor. If you do want to compete, we can talk about it.

The goal is not to make you look like a fighter. It is to make you move like one without having to think about it.

Where the style comes from

  • 2013 to 2020

    Shaolin Kung Fu and Wushu

    Started at around 11 years old under Shifu Chuantong Lin, a former Shaolin disciple from China. Competed in Wushu and won a number of golds, including at the U.S. Capitol Wushu Championships.

  • 2016 to 2020

    Specialised into SanDa and Qin Na

    Moved into SanDa at about 14, still under Shifu Lin. Built a style that combines Chinese kickboxing, striking, throws, takedowns and clinch work. Started teaching weekly SanDa and self defense while still competing.

  • 2020 to 2021

    Kickboxing, Muay Thai and boxing

    The Shaolin school closed during Covid. Kept training through kickboxing and Muay Thai at Impact Martial Arts, with SanDa still the primary art. Boxing became a real part of the game during college, including dedicated boxing work at Blueline, which is where the hands in his SanDa come from.

  • 2021 to 2024

    Black Sash under Edgar Ruiz

    Trained under Coach Edgar Ruiz, a graduate of the Shichahai Sports School in Beijing, and earned a Black Sash in SanDa. This is where the wrestling and takedown integration, clinch work and modern competitive SanDa came together.

  • 2025 to now

    UFC FIT Wayne

    Joined in February 2025 as a private striking coach. Became MMA Lead that August and Assistant MMA Director in September, and built the largest private coaching book in the company.
