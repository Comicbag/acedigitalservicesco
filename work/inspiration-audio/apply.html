Recording application

Free and low-cost recording sessions for independent artists and bands in financial need.

How it works

Inspiration Audio is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to uplifting independent artists and bands in financial need. All donations and payments made towards our recording work go directly back into the nonprofit to support these efforts.

If you are an artist seeking a free or low-cost recording session, complete the form below and we will be in touch to talk about your session.

Questions or concerns? Email Liz Robbins directly at Liz@inspirationaudio.org.

Before you start

It takes about ten minutes. Have links to your music handy if you have any. Nothing here is a test, and every application is read.
