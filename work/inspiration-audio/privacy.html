Privacy policy

What this website collects, why, and who can see it. Written in plain language, because a policy nobody reads protects nobody.

A note while this site is in preview

This is a rebuilt version of inspirationaudio.org, prepared by Ace Digital and not yet published at that address. It describes how the new site works. Inspiration Audio should read it and tell us anything that is wrong before it goes live.

Last updated: 20 September 2026.

Who we are

Inspiration Audio is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit recording studio in Rahway, New Jersey. You can reach us at info@inspirationaudio.org or (908) 900-0229. If you want anything on this page explained, or you want your information removed, that is the address to write to.

What we collect, and only when you give it to us

We do not ask for anything you do not volunteer. There are four forms on this site, and each one collects exactly what it shows you on screen:

  • Contact form. Your first and last name, phone number, email address and your message.
  • Membership inquiry. The same, plus which kind of membership you are asking about.
  • Rent a studio. The same, plus which room you want and the date you are asking for.
  • Scholarship application. This one asks for more, because it has to: your name, phone, email, home address, date of birth, whether you are an artist or an engineer, your school if you are in one, your background, and why you are applying.

If you make an account

You do not need an account to use this site or to send us anything. If you choose to make one, we store your name, email address, and optionally your phone number and instrument. Your password is stored scrambled, in a form nobody here can read, including us.

You can also sign in with Google. If you do, Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture, and nothing else. We never see your Google password. You can disconnect it at any time from your Google account settings.

Signed in, you can see everything you have sent us on your account page, and change or delete your details yourself.

Where it goes, and who reads it

Everything you send goes to a database that Inspiration Audio owns, running on a server that Ace Digital operates and maintains for them. It is not sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you. There are no advertising networks on this site.

The people who can read it are Inspiration Audio staff, and Ace Digital as the company that builds and maintains the site. Nobody else.

Young people and the scholarship form

The scholarship application asks for a date of birth and a home address. We are a music organisation and some of the people who apply are under 18.

If you are under 18, please fill that form in with a parent or guardian. If a young person has sent us something and you would like it removed, email info@inspirationaudio.org and we will delete it.

Other companies whose things appear on this site

  • YouTube hosts the tutorial videos. We embed them in privacy-enhanced mode, which means YouTube does not set tracking cookies unless you press play.
  • Spotify hosts the artist tracks on the Artists page.
  • Givebutter and Donorbox handle donations. Card details are entered on their systems and never touch this website.
  • Google, if you choose to sign in with it.

Each of those has its own privacy policy covering what they do with what they see.

How long we keep things

Enquiries and applications are kept so we can answer them and keep track of who we have helped. Ask us to delete yours and we will. If you have an account, you can delete it yourself, and doing so removes your details.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If you sign in, your browser stores a token so you stay signed in; signing out removes it. The embedded YouTube and Spotify players may set their own cookies once you press play.

Asking us anything

Write to info@inspirationaudio.org. You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, and we will.

Terms of use

This site is provided as it is, for finding out about Inspiration Audio and getting in touch. Sending a form is a request, not a booking or an acceptance: we will always come back to you to confirm. The music, photographs and recordings on this site belong to the artists and photographers who made them and are used with their permission.
